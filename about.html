About Honey Bears Bakery

Welcome to Honey Bears Bakery! We are a family-run bakery based in Johannesburg, started in 2024. What began as baking for family and friends has grown into a small shop loved by our local community.

Our Mission

To Bake Delicious, High-Quality Treats Using Fresh & Natural Ingredients With No Artificial Preservatives, And To Serve Every Customer With A Warm Smile.

Our Values

To be the most trusted local bakery in Gauteng, known for great taste, fair prices, and supporting local suppliers.

Cake Muffins Cookies
Delicious, high-quality cakes made with natural ingredients. Freshly baked muffins, perfect for breakfast or a snack. Soft and chewy cookies, ideal for sharing with family and friends.
